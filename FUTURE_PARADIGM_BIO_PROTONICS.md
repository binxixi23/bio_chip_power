## Ⅱ. Core Engineering Pillars

### 1. Bio-Doped Semiconductor Matrix via Ginseng Pyrolysis
Traditional hardware requires complex ion-implantation (doping) to create charge carrier channels. SBLC v2.0 utilizes **Self-Doped Bio-Graphene Structures** extracted via the pyrolysis of ginseng roots (*Panax ginseng*).
* **The Self-Doping Mechanism:** Natural ginseng roots are rich in **Gintonin** and **Ginsenosides**, complexes densely bound with organic heteroatoms (Nitrogen, Phosphorus) and mobile biogenic ions (K⁺, Ca²⁺, Na⁺).
* **Pyrolysis Dynamics:** Controlled oxygen-free thermal degradation at 1000°C carbonizes the organic scaffolding into an amorphous, high-surface-area (>2000 m²/g) carbon matrix. The residual mineral ions are trapped directly within the carbon sp² lattice, functioning as **natural charge-acceleration benches** that guide localized plasma waves or proton fluxes without artificial chemical doping.

### 2. Nano-Confined Ferroelectric Ice Gates (The Transistor Replacement)
To prevent ice from melting or sublimating in the vacuum of space, water molecules are subject to **Extreme Geometric Confinement**.
* **Nano-Confinement Mechanics:** Ultra-pure H₂O molecules are locked inside the carbonized ginseng nano-channels (diameter < 2 nm). Under Van der Waals forces exerted by the carbon walls, the freezing point of water shifts drastically, allowing the ice crystal lattice to remain **completely stable at room temperature (up to 100°C)**.
* **State Switching (0 and 1):** At sub-zero cryogenic space temperatures or under localized fields, nano-confined ice exhibits **Surface Ferroelectricity**. The hydrogen bonds align to form distinct electrical dipoles. 
  * Applying a positive gate voltage snaps the hydro-bonds to *Orientation-A* (**State 1**).
  * Applying a negative gate voltage flips them to *Orientation-B* (**State 0**).
  State switching operates at the molecular level, requiring near-zero thermodynamic energy dissipation.

## Ⅲ. Performance, Durability, and Security Benchmarks

### 1. Ultra-High Terahertz (THz) Processing Speeds
Silicon computing is bottlenecked at ~5-6 GHz due to Joule heating caused by physical electron friction. 
* **The Grotthuss Cascade:** The ice matrix transmits data using **Proton Currents (H⁺)** via the Grotthuss mechanism. Protons do not physically run across the transistor channel; instead, covalent and hydrogen bonds continuously swap partners down an interconnected chain of water molecules.
* **Execution Latency:** The propagation of hydrogen bond reorganization occurs at the femtosecond scale (10⁻¹⁵ s), pushing theoretical processing frequencies into the **Terahertz (THz) spectrum** (10¹² Hz)—effectively 100x to 1,000x faster than modern silicon limits.

### 2. Self-Healing Durability & Infinite Lifespan
* **Zero Electromigration:** Silicon circuits degrade over time because flowing electron rivers physically wear down copper interconnects. In protonic ice, atoms only vibrate and rotate in fixed spatial positions. There is no material displacement, ensuring zero physical wear-and-tear.
* **Cosmic Ray Immunity:** High-energy cosmic rays (alpha particles, solar protons) cause permanent lattice displacement damage in silicon. In SBLC v2.0, if a cosmic ray hits a sub-channel and melts it, the ambient pressure of the nano-channels combined with the electro-stabilization field triggers **instantaneous electro-freezing**. The channel self-heals in nanoseconds, resulting in an infinite hardware lifecycle.

### 3. Absolute Anti-Reverse Engineering (Hardware Self-Destruct)
SBLC v2.0 offers unprecedented hardware-level security designed to neutralize state-sponsored physical tampering or scanning.
* **Phase-Change Volatility:** The exact architectural pathways and logic gates are dictated by localized electro-stabilization fields and internal pressure gradients. 
* **The Self-Destruct Trigger:** If an adversary attempts to breach the chip housing or dissect it under a Scanning Electron Microscope (SEM) to clone the architecture, the localized pressure seals break and the electrical fields dissolve. **The entire logic network instantly melts into un-patterned water droplets and inert carbon dust within 1 second.**
