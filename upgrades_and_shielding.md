# Engineering Upgrades & Cosmic Shielding Solutions

To transition the SBLC system from a theoretical model to a resilient, operational space asset, the following structural upgrades must be implemented.

## 1. Multi-Layer Radiation Armor (Mitigating Cosmic Rays)
To protect liquid metal charge oscillations from cosmic radiation without drastically increasing weight, the system implements a composite shield:
*   **Outer Layer:** Thin high-density Polyethylene (rich in hydrogen to block and disperse cosmic protons).
*   **Inner Layer:** Carbonized bio-composite sheets to absorb secondary Bremsstrahlung scattering.

## 2. Plasma Pulse Stabilization via External Lorentz Fields
*   **Upgrade:** Instead of relying entirely on external static permanent magnets to guide particles, we deploy dynamic micro-coils along the carbonized bamboo channels [T1].
*   **Mechanism:** Running localized micro-currents generates targeted **Lorentz forces**, instantly correcting any structural shifting or phase separation in the semi-liquid Galinstan matrix without manual maintenance.

## 3. Self-Contained Micro-Fluidic "Water-Chamber" Homeostasis
*   **Upgrade:** To counter structural evaporation or microscopic loss of material over decades, the system introduces a micro-fluidic auxiliary pump loop.
*   **Mechanism:** Integrated nano-sensors monitor channel pressure. If data signal degradation indicates volume loss, the system triggers targeted micro-injections of raw semi-liquid alloy to re-stabilize the logic matrix.
